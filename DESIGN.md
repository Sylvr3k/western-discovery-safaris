## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the spirit of bespoke, remote Tanzanian expeditions along the Western Circuit—anchored in Kigoma, the ancient waters of Lake Tanganyika, and the primeval chimpanzee habitats of Gombe Stream and Mahale Mountains. 

The aesthetic is an authentic fusion of **Tactile Editorial** and **Field Naturalist Romanticism**. It rejects sanitized, generic luxury travel tropes in favor of an evocative, archival aesthetic: weathered parchment tones, typographic hierarchy drawn from vintage botanical expeditions and hand-typed expedition manifests, and geographic detailing such as passport cachets, botanical annotations, and contour route markers.

### Target Audience & Emotional Impact
- **Audience:** Discerning adventure travelers, conservationists, researchers, and safari purists seeking immersive, off-the-beaten-track encounters.
- **Emotional Response:** Inspires wonder, literary curiosity, rugged dependability, deep place-based reverence, and tactile intimacy.

## Colors

The color palette traces a geological and ecological descent: from sun-bleached shores and miombo woodlands down to the depths of Lake Tanganyika and the primeval canopy of Mahale.

### Core Swatches & Application
- **Primary Olive (`#323114`):** Represents sun-cured canvas, dry miombo leaves, and deep bush shade. Used for critical headers, primary call-to-action fills, and primary structural dividing lines.
- **Secondary Bronze Gold (`#8C6A28`):** Evokes brass field compasses, dried acacia pods, and afternoon savannah glare. Serves as an accent for badges, eyebrow tags, key figures, and secondary interactive states.
- **Tertiary Sunset Ochre (`#D95D39`):** Snatched directly from equatorial sunsets dipping behind the Congo mountains across the lake. Deployed sparingly for urgent status items, highlights, route alerts, and seasonal highlights.
- **Neutral Deep Canopy (`#1B2E1E`):** The darkness of equatorial montane rainforest. Used for primary long-form body text, footers, and maximum-contrast structural frames.

### Environmental & Grounding Tones
- **Canvas Khaki (`#F9F5EC`):** The primary page canvas; an organic, unbleached cotton paper base that replaces sterile white.
- **Pressed Parchment (`#F2EBD9`):** Secondary container surface; creates quiet contrast for cards, inset summaries, and logbook entries.
- **Tanganyika Deep Teal (`#164B53`):** Oceanic lake depths; applied to marine excursions, boat transfer modules, and nocturnal journey markers.
- **Ripened Mango (`#E5A93B`):** Warm utility tone used for interactive star ratings, wildlife tags, and attention counters.
- **Equatorial Jungle Green (`#1E3A2B`):** Intermediate forest tone for chimp-trekking itineraries and deep vegetation callouts.

## Typography

The typography unites three distinct voices: the literary authority of early East African exploration journals, the organic human warmth of field reports, and the precise mechanical instrumentation of bush telegraphs and GPS coordinates.

### Font Hierarchy
- **Headlines (`Vollkorn`):** Deep, weighted, and sturdy serif with substantial bracketed serifs and classic proportion. Handles big destination declarations and poetic narrative introductions. It conveys historical permanence without feeling stiff or academic.
- **Body (`Karla`):** A slightly quirky grotesque sans-serif with an irregular, human stroke finish. It keeps dense safari itineraries, packing guides, and conservation briefings effortless to digest across both long desktop readouts and mobile viewports.
- **Labels & Coordinates (`Space Mono`):** Monospaced type used exclusively for timestamps, altitude readings, trail coordinates, passport-style rubber stamps, route numbering, and metadata flags.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors an open field ledger: structured yet spacious, allowing high-resolution imagery and narrative copy to coexist without digital clutter.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1024px+):** Asymmetric 12-column fluid grid framed within a maximum container width of `1380px`. Margins stay generous at `3rem` to mimic editorial margins in hardcover books. Gutters remain set at `1.5rem`.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column grid with `2rem` margins and `1.25rem` gutters. Route sidebars collapse into sticky bottom trays or horizontal drawers.
- **Mobile (< 767px):** 4-column flow with tight `1.25rem` outer margins and `1rem` gutters. Complex multi-column itinerary schedules transition into vertical stepped timelines.

### Spatial Rhythm
Padding relies strictly on the `space-*` scale. Component interiors enforce asymmetrical padding—giving extra air to headers while keeping technical data blocks tightly grouped, honoring editorial conventions.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids digital drop shadows, synthetic blurs, and floating cards. Depth is tactile, physical, and achieved through tonal paper layering, subtle border framing, and ink impressions.

### Layering Philosophy
- **Level 0 (Base Earth):** Primary background (`#F9F5EC`).
- **Level 1 (Field Slip / Card):** Textured surface (`#F2EBD9`) bound by a hairline rule (`1px solid rgba(50, 49, 20, 0.15)`).
- **Level 2 (Active Overlay / Modal):** Deep forest tint (`#1B2E1E`) or parchment inset raised strictly through double-line architectural borders (a 1px solid stroke enclosed inside a 3px offset perimeter) rather than fuzzy blur radii.
- **Stamp & Ink Imprints:** Rubber stamp elements, such as expedition permits or chimp trek permits, sit at a fixed `-2deg` to `+3deg` tilt, visually stamped onto Level 1 surfaces with semi-transparent ink effects (`mix-blend-mode: multiply`).

## Shapes

The shape system is deliberately soft and organic (`roundedness: 1`), evoking hand-trimmed travel documents, field notebooks, cloth patches, and botanical labels rather than sleek software interfaces.

- **Base Corner Radius (`0.25rem`):** Applied to buttons, text fields, cards, and system indicators. Elements retain crisp rectangular presence while eliminating sharp digital corners.
- **Notched & Scalloped Accents:** Specialized components (such as permits, flight vouchers, and itinerary milestones) utilize programmatic corner cutouts (`notch-corner`) and serrated edges to emulate physical torn tickets and perforated safari transit permits.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Background in Primary Olive (`#323114`), text in Canvas Khaki (`#F9F5EC`), set in uppercase `Space Mono` (`label-lg`) with `1px` tracking. Padding: `space-sm` vertical by `space-lg` horizontal. Radius: `0.25rem`. Hover triggers a crisp shift to Secondary Bronze (`#8C6A28`).
- **Secondary / Ghost Button:** Transparent background, `1px solid #323114` border, text in `#323114`. On hover, the background fills with `#F2EBD9`.
- **Expedition Outlined CTA:** Styled with a tactile dash-border (`1.5px dashed #8C6A28`), perfect for booking inquiries and downloadable offline topographic maps.

### Chips & Badges
- **Expedition Stamps:** Enclosed by an imperfect circular or pill-shaped outline with `label-sm` monospaced lettering. Often rotated slightly, utilizing colors like Sunset Ochre (`#D95D39`) or Deep Teal (`#164B53`) with `opacity: 0.85` and `mix-blend-mode: multiply`.
- **Species / Region Tags:** Rectangular chips with `0.25rem` radius, colored in Pressed Parchment (`#F2EBD9`) with a `1px solid rgba(50, 49, 20, 0.2)` border and `#1B2E1E` text.

### Form Controls (Inputs, Checkboxes & Radios)
- **Input Fields:** Crisp bottom-border emphasis or full minimal box (`1px solid rgba(50, 49, 20, 0.25)`), interior filled with subtle `#F2EBD9`. Focus states switch the border to `#8C6A28` with zero outer focus ring, preserving a clean print-journal feel.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Hand-rendered appearance using Primary Olive (`#323114`) borders. Checked states replace modern checks with a solid mechanical cross (`×`) or filled brass square indicator.

### Cards & Itinerary Modules
- **Expedition Day Card:** Built on `#F2EBD9` with an ultra-fine border. Features an editorial header with `Vollkorn` headlines, monospaced coordinate badges at the top-right corner, and body copy in `Karla`.
- **Boat Transfer / Marine Panel:** Draped in Tanganyika Deep Teal (`#164B53`) background with `#F9F5EC` text, accented with hand-drawn SVG bathymetric ripples and bronze route indicators.

### Wayfinding & Route Tracks
- **Hand-Drawn Trail Indicators:** SVG vectors depicting trails across the Mahale ridge or Lake Tanganyika shoreline use irregular dashed strokes (`stroke-dasharray: 4, 3`) in Sunset Ochre (`#D95D39`) punctuated by numbered brass compass waypoints.
