Why Book Us

Gombe Stream National Park is reached from Kigoma, on the shore of Lake Tanganyika, and that's where we're based. Here's what that means for your trip.

01

We're Based in Kigoma

Western Discovery Safaris operates from Kigoma, the lakeside town where trips to Gombe begin. Besides Gombe treks and field programs, we also run tours of Kigoma town, so you can see the place before or after you head into the park.

• BASED IN KIGOMA •
02

Small Groups, Quiet Trek

Chimpanzee viewing in Gombe is regulated to protect the animals, with limits on group size, viewing time and distance. We follow the park's rules and regulations.

• PARK RULES FOLLOWED •
03

The Lake Is Our Road

Gombe is reached by boat from Kigoma, so the trip starts with a journey across Lake Tanganyika before the trek begins. Expect big water, forested hills and a view of the shore you won't get from a road. The view from the water is part of the trip.

• LAKE TANGANYIKA •
04

Built for Researchers, Too

Four of our six packages are designed for students, researchers and conservationists, from Gombe Field Discovery to the Experts Signature Expedition.
