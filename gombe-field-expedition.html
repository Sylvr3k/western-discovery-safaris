Gombe Field Expedition

10 days, 9 nights, up to 12 guests

Stop observing the forest. Start understanding it.

Ten days in Gombe changes how you see it. This is not a quick visit or a trial run. It's real time in the field, with time to learn the rhythm of the forest and the lives of the chimpanzees who call it home.

You'll begin with biodiversity and species identification, then move into research methods and data collection, spending days on chimpanzee social behavior, feeding habits and ranging patterns. You'll dive into Gombe's conservation history and the threats it faces today, and then put that knowledge to use in the field. Later you'll look at the human side, from surrounding communities to sustainable livelihoods, before returning to Kigoma to explore its historical sites and monuments.

You'll finish with a debrief, a certificate, and practical field skills that last long after you leave. Local meals, boat travel, airport transfers and accommodation are all included. Perfect for researchers, conservationists, and anyone who wants ten days instead of three.

Itinerary

  1. Day 1: Arrival and First Observation

    • Airport pick-up, briefing and boat to Gombe.
    • Settle in, then a first trek and behavioral observation session.

  2. Day 2: Forest Biodiversity

    • Species identification, focusing on the primates of Gombe.

  3. Days 3–4: Research Methods and Data Collection

    • Field research techniques, with a focus on chimpanzee studies.

  4. Days 5–6: Chimpanzee Social Behavior

    • Feeding and ranging behavior.
    • Extended observation sessions.

  5. Days 7: Conservation History and Threats

    • Gombe's conservation history and the pressures on the park today.
    • Fieldwork applying what you've learned.

  6. Days 8: Final Deep Field Push

    • Extended time in the field, then data consolidation and analysis.

  7. Days 9: People and Conservation

    • Communities around Gombe, sustainable livelihoods and the challenges of conservation.

  8. Return and Kigoma Historical Sites

    • Boat to Kigoma.
    • Visit historical sites and monuments in Kigoma, with ground transport provided.
    • Group debrief and certificates, then airport transfer.
