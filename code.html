Why Locals Do It Better

Most luxury safari operators in East Africa operate out of glass high-rises in Nairobi or corporate lodges in Arusha. Here on the shores of Lake Tanganyika, our playground is different. We believe in four non-negotiable rules.

01

We Don't Fly In From Nairobi or Arusha

Our guides were born listening to the chimps call across the deep valleys of Kigoma. We know the boat captains, the lake water currents, the park rangers, and every hidden rocky cove where the fish gather at dusk. You don’t need an overseas tour leader when your tracker has known the chimp families since childhood.

• Rooted in Kigoma Slipway •
02

Max 6 Guests, Zero Bullhorns

True wilderness demands absolute silence. When a 50kg alpha chimpanzee bounds past just two meters away down a mossy ridge, you want reverent whispers, not a tour bus crowd jostling for photos. We enforce strict private party caps to safeguard primate well-being.

• Ethological Distance Enforced •
03

The Lake is Our Highway

No dusty corrugations or exhaust-choked safari vans. We travel via traditional wooden dhows crafted by lake carpenters, retrofitted with modern outboard power, shaded sun awnings, life jackets, and cold Kilimanjaro beer waiting in the icebox after a grueling mountain trek.

• Lake Tanganyika Marine Fleet •
04

Fair-Wage Park Trackers

We don’t undercut local knowledge. 100% of our tracking fees go straight into Kigoma community schools, ranger healthcare funds, and direct stipends for the community scouts who patrol the park perimeters against illegal charcoal and snare poaching.

• Community Owned & Operated •
